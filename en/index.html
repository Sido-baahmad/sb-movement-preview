Our philosophy

More than fitness. A way of life.

At SB Movement, performance is never separated from recovery, movement quality and balance. Four pillars shape every session, every program and every experience.

Discover our philosophy
  1. 01

    Performance

    CrossFit, strength, conditioning and functional training, with progress built session after session.

  2. 02

    Movement

    Technique, mobility, flexibility and muscle awareness, to move with precision and control.

  3. 03

    Recovery

    Breathwork, stretching, yoga, sleep and supervised cold exposure. Recovery is part of performance.

  4. 04

    Well-being

    Balance, mental clarity, healthy habits and community, every day and through immersive experiences.
